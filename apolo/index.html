About

We make software we would like to use ourselves. PKBattle came from the need of tournament organizers who were tired of paper brackets and spreadsheets. Body Defense came from the wish to make a tower defense with a clear idea and no clutter: one thumb, one body, one fight.

Our games have no ads and collect nothing from players. Read how Body Defense handles data in its privacy policy.
