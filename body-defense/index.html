How it works

Build

Tap an empty slot along the path to place an immune cell. Tap a cell to sell it.

Earn amino acids

Defeated enemies drop amino acids, your only currency. Build smart and keep the economy going.

Read your enemy

Tap a germ to read its card. Some go dormant and turn immune, some split in two, some resist slowing, some come back from the dead.

One thumb

Played in portrait. Call the next wave early, speed the game up, or pause to think.

10 defenders

Each cell fights in its own way, and the right mix wins levels.
