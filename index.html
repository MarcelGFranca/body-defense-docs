How it works

Build

Tap an empty slot along the path to place an immune cell. Tap a cell to sell it.

Earn amino acids

Defeated enemies drop amino acids, your only currency. Build smart and keep the economy going.

Read your enemy

Tap a germ to read its card. Some go dormant and turn immune, some split in two, some resist slowing, some come back from the dead.

One thumb

Played in portrait. Call the next wave early, speed the game up, or pause to think.

10 defenders

Each cell fights in its own way, and the right mix wins levels.

Questions and answers

What is Body Defense?

Body Defense is a pixel-art tower defense game for Android set inside the human body. Viruses, bacteria and fungi walk along veins and organs, and you place immune cells such as macrophages and lymphocytes to stop them. It has 16 levels, 10 defenders, 23 enemies and 3 difficulties.

Is Body Defense free?

Yes. It is free to download and to play, and it has no in-app purchases. It shows one light ad (Google AdMob) between levels, never during a game.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game plays without an internet connection. A connection is only used to show ads, to send anonymous play data if you agree, and to show the ladders.

Does it have ads?

Yes, a short ad can appear after you win a level and tap to go on. Ads never appear during a game, and not in the first levels. Where the law requires it, for example in Europe and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent first.

What data does it collect?

It needs no account and does not ask for your name or email. The first time you open it, the game asks whether it may send anonymous play data (levels, results, towers used and a random player ID); you can say no, or change it at any time in Settings. The ads, provided by Google AdMob, collect their own data under Google's policy. The game itself does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. All the details are in the privacy policy.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Portuguese, French, Spanish, Italian, Chinese and Japanese.

Which Android versions does it need?

Android 7.0 or newer, on a 64-bit (arm64) phone. The game is played in portrait.

Is it a game for kids?

It is made for players aged 13 and over, and its ads are not directed at children. It is a game, not a lesson or medical advice, but its enemies are real microbes with their real names, so students and curious players can pick up a few facts while they play.

Where can I get it?

Body Defense is coming to Google Play for Android. This page will link to the store as soon as the game is public.

Who made it?

Body Defense is made by Apolo Systems, an independent studio in Brazil.
